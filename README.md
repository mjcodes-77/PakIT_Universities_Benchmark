# PakIT Benchmark

## Why Do Top-Ranked Universities Dominate Pakistan's IT Job Market?

### Institutional Benchmark Analysis

## Overview

This project investigates institutional factors that may be associated
with career outcomes for IT graduates in Pakistan.

The analysis compares selected Pakistani universities using indicators
such as QS ranking, annual academic cost, academic reputation,
employer reputation, citations per paper, and international research
network.

## Research Question

What institutional factors are associated with stronger career outcomes
for IT graduates in Pakistan?

## Objectives

- Compare Pakistani universities across institutional indicators.
- Examine relationships between university ranking and career-related factors.
- Analyze the relationship between academic reputation and employer reputation.
- Investigate the role of institutional cost and research indicators.
- Identify patterns that may help explain differences in IT career outcomes.

## Dataset

The dataset contains institutional-level information for selected
Pakistani universities.

### Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| University Name | Name of the institution |
| Campus Location | Location of the campus |
| QS Ranking | QS World University Ranking 2026 |
| Institution Type | Public or Private |
| Annual Academic Cost | Estimated annual academic cost in PKR |
| Citations per Paper | Research citation indicator |
| Academic Reputation | Academic reputation score |
| International Research Network | International research connectivity |
| Employer Reputation | Employer reputation score |
